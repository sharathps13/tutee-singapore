Why Singapore

Asia's leading place to study, and a launchpad for your career

Singapore pairs globally ranked degrees with short flights home, an English-speaking city, and a clear route from Student's Pass to Employment Pass.

Top-ranked universities

NUS is #10 and NTU #12 in the QS 2027 rankings. SUTD and SMU both climbed sharply this year.

MOE Tuition Grant

A government grant open to international students that substantially lowers tuition, in return for a three-year work bond.

Taught in English

Every course is taught in English, and there is no immigration language test. Your university sets the entry score.

Work while you study

Up to 16 hours a week in term and no cap in vacation, at MOM-approved institutions such as NUS, NTU, SMU and the polytechnics.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Straight answers, checked against ICA, MOM and university sources in October 2026.

AdmissionsWhat do I need to apply?

Most universities ask for:

  • A valid passport and your academic transcripts
  • An English test score (IELTS, TOEFL or PTE Academic)
  • A statement of purpose and references

Singapore has no immigration language test; your university sets the score. Some postgraduate courses, MBAs especially, also ask for a GMAT and work experience.

AdmissionsWhen should I apply?

The autonomous universities start in August. For August 2027, NUS accepts international applications from mid-December 2026 to mid-February 2027, and the others open around the same time. Private institutions and polytechnics have more intakes. Start about a year ahead so there's time for tests and documents.

Student's PassHow does the Student's Pass process work?

After you accept an offer, your institution registers you in SOLAR and you complete the application online.

  • Apply 2 to 3 months before your course starts
  • ICA usually decides within 1 week, or 2 weeks if you need a visa
  • You pay a processing fee when you apply and an issuance fee when the Pass is issued

We confirm the current ICA fees with you during your consultation.

Student's PassDo I need a medical exam or interview?

ICA may ask for a medical examination report, depending on your case, and you complete formalities in Singapore after arrival. There is no visa interview for the Student's Pass. We prepare you for each step before you fly.

MoneyWhat is the Tuition Grant, and what's the catch?

It's a Singapore government grant, open to international students, that substantially lowers tuition at the autonomous universities and polytechnics. In return you sign a bond to work for a Singapore-registered employer for three years after graduating. Your advisor shows you exact fees with and without the grant for your course.

MoneyWhat does it cost to study and live in Singapore?

It depends on your course, your housing and your lifestyle, and fees are revised every year. Rather than publish figures that go out of date, we build a personal cost plan with you in your free consultation, covering tuition, housing, living costs and how to fund them.

MoneyCan I get an education loan?

Yes. Through our banking partners we arrange collateral and non-collateral education loans covering tuition, accommodation and living costs. We'll show you the repayment plan before you sign.

Work and afterCan I work part-time while I study?

Only at institutions on the Ministry of Manpower's approved list, which includes NUS, NTU, SMU and the polytechnics. There you can work up to 16 hours a week in term and without a limit in official vacations. Most private institutions are not on the list, and exchange students can't work.

Work and afterWhat happens after I graduate?

Singapore has no dedicated post-study work visa. Graduates of an Institute of Higher Learning can apply for a Long-Term Visit Pass to look for work, but can't work on it. Once hired, you move to an Employment Pass.

The Employment Pass needs a qualifying salary and 40 points on MOM's COMPASS framework. The salary criteria rise on 1 January 2027, and we'll walk you through the current figures for your field.

Work and afterCan my family join me?

Not on a Student's Pass. Once you hold an Employment Pass or S Pass, your spouse and children under 21 can join on a Dependant's Pass if you meet the salary criteria. Parents can come on a Long-Term Visit Pass at a higher salary level. We check your family's eligibility in your consultation.
