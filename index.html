Why Singapore?

Singapore is Asia's leading destination for international students, and the only country in Asia with a university in the QS global top ten. It provides students from all streams with an opportunity to learn and succeed in their chosen subjects that is unmatched.

With the MOE Tuition Grant open to international students, an English-medium education and a globally respected qualification, it is the ideal location for students wishing to study and build a career after graduation.

Earn While You Learn: Pay Off Your Loan

We don't just secure your Pass; we tell you the truth about working here. Term-time work is a right only at the universities, polytechnics and ITE on the Ministry of Manpower's specified list — students at most private institutions may not work at all. Where you are eligible, Tutee Connect helps you find permitted work, earn in Singapore dollars, and start on your education loan before you even graduate.

Earn in Singapore dollars, cover your expenses, and start repaying your loan — where your institution qualifies.

Part-Time Job Assistance

We provide explicit assistance upon your arrival to help you secure permitted part-time work — up to 16 hours a week in term time, and without an hourly cap during vacation, if your institution is on the Ministry of Manpower's specified list.

Competitive Hourly Wages

Roles such as retail assistant, food and beverage crew and student ambassador typically pay about S$10–S$15/hr. Singapore sets no national minimum wage; sector Progressive Wage rates apply instead.

The Pathway to Settlement

Singapore offers structured pathways for international students to transition into the workforce and eventually settle permanently. With our expert guidance, navigating these steps becomes straightforward and stress-free.

Singapore Student's Pass

Complete your study program. We help you choose courses that lead straight into a Singapore career.

Long-Term Visit Pass

Graduates of an Institute of Higher Learning can stay a year to look for work. You may not work on it — it is a job-search pass.

Employment Pass

Transition smoothly with our guidance to an employer-sponsored Employment Pass — from S$5,600 a month, and 40 COMPASS points.

Permanent Residence

Take the final step towards settlement — apply for PR once you are established on an Employment Pass or S Pass.

Relocating with Your Family?

We understand the importance of family, so we will not oversell this. A Singapore Student's Pass carries no dependant privileges at all. The routes that work open once you hold an Employment Pass or S Pass — and we plan that sequence with you from the start.

  • Student's Pass:No dependants. Family visit Singapore on their own Visit Pass while you study.
  • Employment Pass Families:Spouse and children under 21 on a Dependant's Pass, from S$6,000 a month.
  • Expert Guidance:We map the Employment Pass and Dependant's Pass sequence with you from the start.

Generally, you need a valid passport, strong academic transcripts, proof of English language proficiency (IELTS, TOEFL or PTE Academic), academic references, and a clear statement of purpose. Singapore sets no immigration language test — the requirement comes from the institution, not from ICA. Some postgraduate courses — MBAs in particular — may also ask for a GMAT score or work experience.

Once you accept an offer, your institution issues a Registration Acknowledgement Letter and registers you in SOLAR. You then complete the Student's Pass application online, and ICA issues an In-Principle Approval. Apply at least two months and no more than three months before your course begins. On arrival you complete formalities by appointment — biometrics and a medical examination report — and pay the S$60 issuance fee, plus S$30 for a Multiple Journey Visa if you need one. There is no credibility interview. We provide comprehensive guidance for every step.

Not while you are studying, and we would rather say so plainly. A Student's Pass carries no dependant privileges — your family would visit on their own Visit Pass. The routes open after you graduate: once you hold an Employment Pass or S Pass and earn at least S$6,000 a month, your spouse and children under 21 can join you on a Dependant's Pass, and a Dependant's Pass holder can work with a Letter of Consent.

Singapore has no dedicated post-study work visa. If you graduate from an Institute of Higher Learning you can apply for a one-year Long-Term Visit Pass to look for a job — but you may not work on it, and you must convert to a proper work pass before starting. The usual destination is the Employment Pass, which needs at least S$5,600 a month (S$6,200 in financial services) and 40 COMPASS points. If you took the MOE Tuition Grant you are already committed to three years with a Singapore-registered employer, which in practice makes this step much easier.
